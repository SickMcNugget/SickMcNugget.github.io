## Dagger throw
You play as some cursed character that can throw a dagger and then control it with their mind.
The camera follows the dagger throw the air when you throw it, and there's a little picture-in-picture display of the character's face as they make different expressions as you control the dagger and they become more gaunt the longer the dagger flies for. It would be a lightweight puzzle game where the goal is to reach the person/creature that cursed you in the first place and kill them with the dagger, releasing the curse. It kind of gives me a Hot Wheels Jetz kind of vibe, with more dark/sinister atmosphere.
Some gameplay ideas:
- Throwing dagger through tight spaces
- Collect energy that prevents character from dying for longer (+time)
- split dagger in two and control both simultaneously (split-screen, up to 4 daggers). Note that each dagger is controlled by the same inputs, so the environment would have to create dynamic gameplay here.
- Some levels end with assassinating a target, some end with cutting a rope, some end with hitting a button.
- Dagger changes trail/look throughout the game to impress progress upon player
- velocity change, updraft, downdraft-style effects.
